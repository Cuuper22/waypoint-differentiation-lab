## During Reading
- Before reading: purpose and vocabulary: Give Learner 7A a four-word preview card. For each word, say it, show a student-friendly meaning, and ask them to choose the matching example before the full text starts.
- Paragraphs 1-2: claim and criteria: Replace open annotation with a two-symbol code: box Lowe's claim and star three traits a definition of community must explain.
- During reading question C for paragraphs 1-2: Ask the original question, then offer a response frame and selectable traits if Learner 7A stalls for more than 10 seconds.
- Paragraphs 3-7: Newcastle example: Use a two-column bridge chart: left side 'Newcastle detail from text,' right side 'How it proves shared story.' Complete the first row together.
- Paragraphs 8-11: why the definition matters: Give a two-column evidence sorter labeled 'What community is' and 'Why community matters.' Learner 7A places two highlighted details before writing.

## Independent Practice
- Transition into independent practice: Before independent work, do a quiet one-on-one check-in: repeat directions, point to the checklist, and agree on a first step. Use specific positive praise for starting.
- Independent short response: Keep the same prompt and evidence expectation, but provide a claim-evidence-explain frame and allow oral rehearsal before writing.

## Student-Facing Materials

### Four-word preview card
- aspect = one part
- moral = about right and wrong
- narrative = story
- specific = exact
- Circle one word you expect to see in a definition of community.

### Two-symbol annotation key
- Box = Lowe's claim or definition.
- Star = detail that explains what makes a community different from a regular group.

### Paragraph 2 trait frame
- A community can be different from a regular group because ____.
- A community can make people feel ____.
- A community can shape what people think is ____ or ____.

### Newcastle bridge chart

- Text detail: ________. How it proves shared story: ________.

### Paragraph 8-11 evidence sorter

- Why community matters: ________
- Best detail for my answer: ________

### First-step check-in card

- Check-back point: after question 2.

- Evidence 1: In paragraph __, he says ________.

### Evidence finder discussion role
